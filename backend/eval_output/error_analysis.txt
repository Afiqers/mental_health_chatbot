--- Discussion ---
The dominant confusion is typically DEPRESSION <-> SUICIDAL: both share vocabulary of hopelessness and worthlessness, so the boundary is genuinely fuzzy even for humans. This is precisely why the system keeps a deterministic crisis safety net rather than trusting the classifier alone for self-harm.
